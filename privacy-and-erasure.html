Finding RehabAI privacy information and requesting data erasure

By RehabAI · September 28, 2026

RehabAI’s website provides two dedicated destinations for data questions: the privacy policy and the erasure request page. Here is how to find and use them.

Where is the privacy policy?

The RehabAI privacy policy is linked in the main navigation. It describes the information RehabAI collects, how it is used and retained, and how to contact RehabAI with questions. Read that page for the full policy and its last-updated date.

How do I request data erasure?

Open the erasure request page and follow the link to the Google Form. The page also provides an email option if you prefer to send your request directly.

The request page explains that the supplied information is used to identify your account and review your request. Follow the instructions there when submitting account details.

Does submitting the form delete my data immediately?

No. Submitting the form starts a manual review. RehabAI reviews the request and responds by email; the form does not automatically erase account data. See the request page and policy for the applicable conditions.

Why document this workflow?

A visible request link is only useful when people understand what happens next. The website keeps the request instructions, contact option, and policy accessible from the same navigation, so visitors can move between them without searching through the app preview.

This article explains the existing website workflow. It does not change the privacy policy or introduce a new deletion feature.
