Your right to request erasure

You may request that we delete personal data associated with you from our app and systems, subject to any legal obligations or exceptions that require or permit us to retain certain information.

You do not have to use this form to submit a deletion request. However, providing the information below will help us identify your account and process your request efficiently.

Information provided through this form will be used only to verify, review, and respond to your request and for any related legal or recordkeeping requirements.

01 Your information
02 Who is making this request?

We may ask for reasonable evidence that you are authorized to act on the person's behalf.

03 Identity verification

We need to make sure that personal data is not deleted at the request of an unauthorized person.

Whenever reasonably possible, we will verify your identity using information already associated with your account. If we cannot reasonably verify your identity, we may request additional information necessary to process the request.

Any additional information collected for verification will be used for that purpose and handled in accordance with our privacy practices.

04 What would you like us to delete?

05 What happens next?

After receiving your request, we will:

  1. Confirm that we received your request.
  2. Verify your identity when reasonably necessary.
  3. Review the personal data and systems covered by your request.
  4. Delete personal data when required by applicable law and our policies.
  5. Notify you when the request has been completed or explain why some or all of the requested data cannot be deleted.

Submitting this form does not mean that your data is immediately deleted. Your request is considered complete once we have processed the request and provided confirmation of its completion.

We will respond within the time required by applicable privacy law.

06 Information we may need to retain

In some circumstances, we may be required or permitted to retain certain information even after receiving a deletion request. For example, information may need to be retained to:

  • Comply with a legal or regulatory obligation;
  • Prevent or investigate fraud, security incidents, or misuse;
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims;
  • Maintain records of privacy requests and our responses;
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users or others; or
  • Satisfy another exception permitted by applicable law.

Where appropriate, we will inform you if we cannot delete some or all of the requested information.
