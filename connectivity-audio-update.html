Clearer connection errors and stronger audio

By RehabAI · September 29, 2026

This RehabAI update makes the app more reliable when internet access is unavailable and makes spoken guidance easier to hear.

Clear feedback when there is no internet

The app now handles missing internet access with a clear error state. Instead of leaving users unsure about what happened, RehabAI can explain that a connection is unavailable so they know why online functionality cannot continue.

More consistent sound when the app opens

RehabAI now sets the sound volume to 60% when entering the app. This addresses cases where spoken instructions were too quiet and gives users a more consistent starting volume for audio guidance.

Why these changes matter

Exercise guidance needs to be understandable and dependable. Clear connection messages reduce confusion, while a consistent audio level helps users hear instructions without first troubleshooting quiet sound.

We will continue improving reliability and the at-home exercise experience in future updates.
