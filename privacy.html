RehabAI is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how RehabAI collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our website or application.

What data do we collect?

RehabAI collects the following data:

Personal information

  • Email address
  • User ID

Health and fitness information

  • Fitness information, such as exercise or physical activity information that you choose to provide

App activity

  • Information about how you interact with our application, such as pages or sections you visit or interact with

App information and performance

  • Crash logs
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as loading time, latency, and other technical information

We do not collect your name, phone number, address, precise location, photos, videos, financial information, messages, contacts, web browsing history, or other categories of personal information listed above unless we specifically notify you and obtain any required permission.

How do we collect your data?

You directly provide RehabAI with most of the data we collect. We collect data when you:

  • Create or register for an account.
  • Provide your email address or other account information.
  • Use features of our application that collect fitness or exercise information.
  • Interact with different parts of our application.
  • Provide information voluntarily while using the application.

We may also automatically collect certain technical information, including crash logs and diagnostic information, when you use our application.

RehabAI does not currently receive personal data indirectly from third-party sources.

How will we use your data?

RehabAI collects your data so that we can:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Identify your account using your user ID.
  • Provide and maintain the application's features.
  • Use fitness information to provide relevant app functionality.
  • Understand how users interact with our application and improve the user experience.
  • Monitor application performance.
  • Identify, investigate, and fix crashes, bugs, and technical problems.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of our application.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How do we store your data?

RehabAI stores your data using secure cloud-based services and takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

RehabAI will retain your personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When your information is no longer necessary, we will delete or anonymize it using reasonable measures.

Marketing

RehabAI does not currently use your personal information to send you marketing communications or share your information with partner companies for marketing purposes.

If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and obtain any consent required by applicable law.

What are your data protection rights?

RehabAI would like to make sure you are fully aware of your data protection rights. Depending on applicable law, you may have the following rights:

The right to access

You may request copies of the personal data we hold about you.

The right to rectification

You may request that we correct information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.

The right to erasure

You may request that we delete your personal data, subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions.

The right to restrict processing

You may request that we restrict the processing of your personal data under certain circumstances.

The right to object to processing

You may have the right to object to certain processing of your personal data.

The right to data portability

Where applicable, you may request that we transfer certain personal data to another organization or directly to you.

If you make a request, we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Contact us about your data:

Email: support@rehab-ai.app

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can be used to remember preferences, maintain sessions, and understand how users interact with a website.

RehabAI may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for the operation and functionality of our website.

How do we use cookies?

RehabAI may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Keeping you signed in.
  • Remembering your preferences.
  • Maintaining necessary website functionality.
  • Understanding how users interact with our website.
  • Improving website performance and user experience.

What types of cookies do we use?

Our website may use the following types of cookies:

Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for basic website functions, such as maintaining your login session and allowing important features to operate.

Functionality cookies

These cookies may remember choices and preferences you make so that we can provide a more personalized experience.

Analytics cookies

Where used, these cookies help us understand how users interact with our website and identify areas where we can improve.

RehabAI does not currently use cookies for targeted advertising.

How to manage cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your web browser's settings. However, disabling certain cookies may cause some website features to function incorrectly.

Privacy policies of other websites

RehabAI's website may contain links to other websites. Our Privacy Policy applies only to our website and application.

If you click a link to another website, we encourage you to review that website's privacy policy. RehabAI is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Changes to our privacy policy

RehabAI keeps this Privacy Policy under regular review and may update it from time to time.

Any updates will be posted on this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

This Privacy Policy was last updated on September 7, 2026.

Email: support@rehab-ai.app

How to contact the appropriate authority

If you have a concern about how RehabAI handles your personal information and we are unable to resolve your concern, you may have the right to contact the appropriate data protection authority in your country or region.

For users in the United States, the appropriate authority may depend on the state and the nature of the complaint. You may also contact your state's relevant consumer protection or privacy regulator.
