Building the RehabAI website: from policy page to app preview

By RehabAI · September 28, 2026

RehabAI’s public website began with a privacy policy. Its repository now documents a broader site with app previews, a video, and a route for requesting data erasure. This post explains that progression.

Starting with information people need

The earliest website commits added the privacy policy and introduced the purple RehabAI accent. The site then expanded into separate app, privacy, and erasure pages, making each destination directly accessible.

Separate pages let visitors link to the information they need. Someone looking for the app can start at the homepage; someone with a data question can go straight to the policy or request page.

Adding a clearer view of the app

September 2026 changes replaced screenshot placeholders with app images, adjusted their presentation to preserve their proportions, and added an embedded YouTube video. The homepage also identifies MIT App Inventor as the build platform and includes the existing Braze partnership credit.

Read our app preview walkthrough for a description of the visible exercise controls and feedback display. The website history records presentation changes; it does not provide an app release number or a verified launch date.

Making updates easier to follow

This website update introduces a blog with individual article pages, descriptive page titles, and links between related topics. The articles are available as ordinary HTML, so visitors can read them without running JavaScript.

Each post includes a publication date and a clear topic. App previews are labeled as previews, and website changes are described separately from app releases. That distinction helps readers understand what has actually changed.

How to follow or suggest changes

Browse the RehabAI blog for published updates. Questions or corrections can be sent to RehabAI by email.

Source: the public website commit history, reviewed September 28, 2026.
